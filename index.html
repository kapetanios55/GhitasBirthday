Before you enquire

Why October works

October sits between the summer wedding season and Christmas party bookings, so most of these venues have realistic availability for a Friday or Saturday — but castle wedding calendars fill 9–12 months out, so enquiring 6–8 weeks ahead is cutting it close for the popular weekend dates. Weekdays are far easier to secure on short notice.

Three different products

"Private dinner hire" venues (including the Natural History Museum) rent a room for an evening — you or a caterer brings the meal. "Whole-castle rental" venues are booked as a self-contained house party, usually a 2–3 night minimum, with dinner cooked in-house for whoever is staying. "Themed dining" is a fixed ticketed experience with no separate room to negotiate. Decide which one your friend actually wants before requesting quotes.

Reading the prices

Figures marked with a green dot are rates published on the venue's own site or a booking platform. Amber-dot figures are estimates built from comparable venues, since most castles price events bespoke and only quote after a call — treat those as a starting point for negotiation, not a number to budget to exactly.

What's missing from a quote

Venue hire rarely includes catering, drinks, flowers, entertainment or a marquee if the dining room is smaller than your group. Ask each venue for an all-in quote for 40 covers rather than the bare hire fee, and confirm whether VAT and a discretionary service charge are already included.

Is the food included?

Every card carries a food badge for exactly this. Food not included means the figure is a venue-hire fee with catering costed separately (the per-head estimate already adds a catering guess on top, but it's not a venue quote). Food included means the published price is genuinely all-in. Food not confirmed means the venue has no published rate at all, so I can't tell you either way — ask directly.
